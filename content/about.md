Hi, I'm Mrs Lee G. On Instagram and YouTube that is my name. Off camera it is Linda.

I have always lived between structure and spark. As a teenager I restyled my Myspace profile for hours and wanted to be a fashion designer or a graphic artist. I ended up in tech, and somewhere along the way the colour came back: a hot pink sewing machine, pencils everywhere, juggling balls next to the laptop.

## What I do here

- **I juggle.** Balls, and everything else. Juggling is how I think about focus and load, and how I explain tech on camera.
- **I sew.** Self-taught since early 2025. My first project was a princess dress, because why start small.
- **I draw.** Eyes, half portraits, pencil studies. Rarely finished.
- **I talk.** On stages and in community meetups, about tech, AI and learning out loud.

## Why "Mrs Lee G"

Because a name with a bit of a wink suits someone who keeps several things in the air at once. I never drop the important one.
