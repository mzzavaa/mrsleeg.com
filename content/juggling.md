My handle is a juggling joke, and juggling is my favourite metaphor for real life: cognitive load, context switching, too many things at once. I code while juggling. Literally.

## Explaining tech while juggling

On my YouTube channel I explain tech topics while three balls are in the air. It started as speaking practice: you cannot juggle and ramble at the same time. It became a format. When I looked at my filler words afterwards, the champion was a "so" at the start of a sentence. So.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@mrs_lee_g)

## A juggler who looks like me

Juggling Lab, the juggling pattern animator, only had one juggler figure, and it was male. So I added a female juggler avatar to my fork of it, with arms that do not disappear behind the dress when they cross, and one avatar per juggler in passing patterns.
