## The pink machine

In early 2025 a hot pink **Singer 3223** arrived, together with a tower of packages full of thread, rotary cutters, scissors, chalk, needles and pins. I could not sew. That was the whole point.

Most of my hobbies start the same way: get the tools, make space, jump in. A few months later there was a teal satin princess dress with a corset bodice on the dress form.
