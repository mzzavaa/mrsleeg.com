Juggling is my favourite way to think about focus. Three balls are fine. Add a fourth while the pattern is running and you feel, within seconds, what it means to add load to a system that was working a moment ago.

That is why the balls live next to my laptop. I code while juggling. Literally.

More about juggling, and why I explain tech while juggling, is on the [juggling page](/juggling/).
