When I was younger I wanted to be a fashion designer or a graphic designer. I drew half-finished people, sketched clothes I would never sew (until recently), and obsessed over eyes and faces.

Half portraits are kind of my thing. Most of the time I do not finish a whole face, and I have made peace with that. Drawing is not about perfection for me. It is how I translate a feeling I cannot quite say in words.

![A pencil portrait of a woman with long dark hair](/images/drawing-portrait.jpg)

Everything here is self-taught: countless hours of trial and error, impulsive sketchbook buys, video rabbit holes and a lot of crumpled paper. I draw to think, I draw when I cannot code anymore, and I draw when the world is too loud.

The lesson I keep relearning: you do not have to know what you are doing to keep doing it.
